HOW SCORES WORK

  1. Sign in without giving up your key. Use a Nostr browser extension or signer app. The arcade never sees your private key.
  2. Play a ranked run. The referee hands your game a sealed random seed before the first move.
  3. Post it. The referee replays every frame of your run with the same rules, works out the score itself, and signs it.

Scores are verified by the arcade referee.
