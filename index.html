    How scores work

    You can post as a guest with a nickname, or sign in to keep your scores on every device.

    1. Start a run. The referee hands your game a sealed random seed before the first move.
    2. Post your score. At game over, tap Post. Guests type a nickname first.
    3. The referee replays it. Every frame of your run is replayed with the same rules, and the referee signs the score it works out itself.

    Sign in once for every game

    Sign-in uses Nostr. The arcade never asks for, sees or stores your private key.

    Scores are verified by the arcade referee.
